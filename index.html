Payroll & reports

Payroll

You know the net. So do we.

When somebody joins an Egyptian company, the only figure they actually know is what lands in their account. Ask them for a gross and HR invents one by adding back deductions nobody can name — and the invented number then gets treated as a fact.

Net 14,200.00 − Deductions 1,200.00 = Payable 13,000.00

That is the whole sheet. The net is what you agreed with the person. The deductions are the ones you recorded — an advance, a penalty, unpaid days. The payable is the subtraction.

  • Rakeez does not calculate income tax, social insurance or end-of-service. Your accountant does that, and Rakeez records the result.
  • Nothing derives a gross from a net, or a net from a gross. A figure a human typed is data; a figure the software guessed is a liability.
  • What you do get: the sheet, the payslip, the history, the audit trail, and the Excel export in the shape your accountant asked for.

Re-run March in July. You get March.

A salary is a dated series, not a current value. Drag the month and watch the same person's figure stay what it was at the time.

Your data

Salaries and national IDs. We treat them that way.

One tenant, one boundary

Every record carries your company and every query is scoped to it. Not a convention — a rule enforced in the data layer, with a test on every model proving one company cannot read another's rows.

Documents behind expiring links

Contracts, IDs and certificates are never served from a guessable address. Links are signed, time-limited, and only issued to someone entitled to the file.

History that isn't quietly rewritten

Salaries, contracts and approvals are dated series. Changing today's figure never edits last year's, and the record of who changed what stays.

Money stored as money

Every amount is held in piastres as a whole number, never a decimal a rounding error can reach. A payroll product that loses half a piastre is a payroll product you cannot use.
